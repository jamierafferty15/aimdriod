How optional AI review handles evidence

After Core review, CRTwise assesses whether optional AI review is likely to add value. You can complete the review using Core findings alone.

If you choose AI review, CRTwise prepares targeted evidence extracts locally. Study-specific protected terms and local privacy screening help identify information that needs reviewer attention. Reviewers inspect the exact payloads and explicitly approve eligible information before transmission.

Approved evidence is sent through an authenticated HTTPS gateway. Each assessment is processed independently. Standalone payloads are not automatically anonymous, so screening and reviewer approval remain essential.

The CRTwise gateway is designed not to persist approved payload bodies or provider response bodies in its operational database. Limited operational metadata supports licensing, auditability and troubleshooting. Provider processing and retention are governed separately by the provider terms and applicable data-processing arrangements.

Human Review Workbook

Evidence to investigate.
Decisions you control.

Following the Core review, CRTwise automatically generates a structured Human Review Workbook (HRW) containing:

  • Identified findings and supporting evidence
  • Review coverage and any limitations
  • Batch Review and Evidence Detail sheets
  • Fields for reviewer decisions and comments
  • The ability to carry forward eligible reviewer decisions from previous reviews
HUMAN OVERSIGHT, THROUGHOUT

The reviewer remains in control.

CRTwise identifies potential issues and provides evidence to support their assessment. It does not automatically modify the submission package.

The reviewer determines which findings require action, which can be accepted as-is, and what changes, if any, should be made.
